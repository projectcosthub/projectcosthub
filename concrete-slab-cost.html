How Much Does a Concrete Slab Cost?

The cost of a concrete slab depends on several factors, including the size of the project, concrete thickness, material prices, labor, site preparation, location and finish.

A simple slab may have a relatively straightforward installation, while projects requiring excavation, grading, reinforcement or difficult access can cost more.

Concrete Slab Cost Per Square Foot

Concrete slab projects are often estimated using a cost per square foot. However, the actual price can vary depending on the project specifications and local market.

Material, labor, thickness, reinforcement, site preparation and finish can all influence the final cost per square foot.

Factors That Affect Concrete Slab Cost

Project Size

The total square footage determines how much concrete and labor may be required. Larger slabs generally require more materials and installation work.

Concrete Thickness

A thicker slab requires more concrete. Changing the thickness can therefore have a significant effect on material requirements.

Site Preparation

Excavation, grading, leveling, removal of existing surfaces and preparation of the base can increase the total project cost.

Reinforcement

Some concrete slab projects may require reinforcement such as wire mesh or rebar depending on the intended use and project specifications.

Labor

Installation costs may include forming, preparation, pouring, leveling and finishing the concrete.

Location

Concrete material and labor costs can vary between states and local markets. Delivery distance and contractor rates can also affect the final price.

Finish

A basic finish generally costs less than decorative finishes. Special textures, coloring or other design options can increase the project cost.

Concrete Slab Cost by Project Type

Garage Slab

Garage slabs may require additional preparation, reinforcement and specifications depending on the structure and intended vehicle loads.

Shed Slab

A shed slab may be smaller than other concrete projects, although preparation and access can still affect the cost.

Patio Slab

A patio slab can vary significantly in size and finish. Decorative finishes may increase the overall project cost.

Foundation Slab

Foundation-related concrete work can involve engineering, reinforcement, excavation and other requirements. Project specifications should be confirmed with qualified professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete slab cost?

The cost depends on size, thickness, materials, labor, preparation, reinforcement, location and finish. Use the calculator above for a general planning estimate.

How much concrete do I need for a slab?

The amount of concrete depends on the length, width and thickness. The calculator converts these measurements into an estimated number of cubic yards.

Does slab thickness affect cost?

Yes. A thicker slab requires more concrete and may also require different preparation or reinforcement depending on the project.

Does site preparation increase concrete slab cost?

Yes. Excavation, grading, removal of existing surfaces and base preparation can increase the overall project cost.

Does location affect concrete slab prices?

Yes. Material, delivery and labor prices can vary between states and local markets.

Are online concrete slab estimates exact?

No. Online calculators provide planning estimates rather than contractor quotes. Actual costs depend on local prices, site conditions, labor, materials and project specifications.

How Much Does House Painting Cost?

The cost of painting a house depends on the total paint area, whether the project is interior or exterior, surface condition, paint quality, labor rates and location.

Additional preparation, repairs, trim work, multiple coats and difficult access can increase the final project cost.

What Affects House Painting Cost?

Project Size

Larger painting projects generally require more materials and labor.

Interior or Exterior

Exterior painting can involve additional preparation, weather-related considerations and access requirements.

Surface Preparation

Cleaning, sanding, scraping, patching and repairing damaged surfaces can increase the overall cost.

Paint Quality

Higher-quality paints can have higher material costs but may offer different coverage and durability characteristics.

Number of Coats

Some projects may require multiple coats to achieve the desired appearance and coverage.

Location

Local labor rates, material prices and market conditions can affect painting project costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to paint a house?

The cost depends on the paint area, project type, surface condition, paint quality, labor and location. Use the calculator above for a general planning estimate.

Is interior or exterior painting more expensive?

Either type can vary significantly in cost depending on the size, preparation requirements, materials and accessibility of the project.

Does surface preparation increase painting costs?

Yes. Repairs, sanding, scraping, cleaning and other preparation work can increase labor requirements.

Does location affect painting prices?

Yes. Labor and material costs can vary between states and local markets.

Are painting calculator estimates exact?

No. The calculator provides a general planning estimate rather than a contractor quote. Actual costs should be confirmed with local painting professionals.

Planning Tip:

Before starting a painting project, compare several local contractor quotes and confirm the exact preparation work, number of coats, materials and surfaces included.

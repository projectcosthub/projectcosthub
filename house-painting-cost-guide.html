How Much Does It Cost to Paint a House?

The cost of painting a house depends on several factors, including the total paintable area, whether the project is interior or exterior, paint quality, surface condition, labor rates and location.

Smaller painting projects may cost a few thousand dollars, while larger homes or projects requiring extensive preparation can cost significantly more.

What Affects House Painting Costs?

Project Size

The amount of surface that needs to be painted is one of the most important factors affecting cost. Larger areas generally require more paint, materials and labor.

Interior vs. Exterior Painting

Interior and exterior painting projects can have different labor requirements and preparation needs. Exterior projects may also involve additional access and weather considerations.

Paint Quality

Paint products are available at different price levels. Higher-quality paints may increase material costs and can have different coverage and durability characteristics.

Surface Condition

Walls and other surfaces that require repairs, scraping, sanding, cleaning or priming may require additional labor before painting begins.

Number of Coats

Some projects may require more than one coat to achieve the desired appearance and coverage. Additional coats can increase both material and labor requirements.

Trim and Detail Work

Doors, windows, baseboards, trim, shutters and other detailed areas can require additional preparation and painting work.

Location

Painting labor rates and material prices can vary between states and local markets. Local demand and availability can also influence project costs.

Accessibility

High ceilings, multiple stories, difficult access and other site conditions can increase labor and equipment requirements.

Interior House Painting Cost

Interior painting costs can depend on the number of rooms, wall and ceiling area, surface condition, number of coats, trim work and paint quality.

Furniture moving, wall repairs, sanding, patching and additional preparation may also affect the final cost.

Exterior House Painting Cost

Exterior painting can involve additional preparation such as power washing, scraping, sanding, caulking and priming.

The type of exterior surface can also affect the project. Wood siding, stucco, brick and other materials may require different preparation and painting methods.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to paint a house?

The cost depends on the size of the project, interior or exterior surfaces, paint quality, labor, preparation and location. Use the calculator above for a general estimate.

Is it cheaper to paint a house yourself?

Painting a house yourself can reduce labor costs, but the project may require tools, preparation, equipment and significant time. Larger or more complex projects may benefit from professional installation.

Does surface preparation increase painting costs?

Yes. Repairs, sanding, scraping, cleaning, patching and priming can increase the amount of labor required.

Does paint quality affect the cost?

Yes. Different paint products have different material costs. Premium products may cost more than basic options.

Does location affect house painting prices?

Yes. Labor rates, material prices and local market conditions can vary between states and local areas.

How much does exterior house painting cost?

Exterior painting costs depend on surface area, exterior material, preparation, accessibility, paint quality, labor and location.

Are house painting calculator estimates exact?

No. Online calculators provide general planning estimates rather than contractor quotes. Actual costs should be confirmed with local painting professionals.

Planning Tip:

Before starting a painting project, compare several local contractor quotes and confirm the surfaces being painted, preparation work, number of coats, materials and cleanup included in each quote.

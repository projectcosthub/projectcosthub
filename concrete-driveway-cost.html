How Much Does a Concrete Driveway Cost?

The cost of a concrete driveway depends on several factors, including the size of the driveway, concrete thickness, material prices, labor, site preparation, location and finish.

A larger driveway generally requires more concrete and more labor, while difficult site conditions or decorative finishes can increase the overall project cost.

Factors That Affect Concrete Driveway Cost

Driveway Size

The larger the driveway, the more concrete and labor will generally be required. Length and width are used to calculate the total project area in square feet.

Concrete Thickness

A thicker driveway requires more concrete. The thickness can have a significant effect on the amount of material required for the project.

Material Costs

Concrete material prices can vary depending on the location, supplier, delivery distance and project size.

Labor and Installation

Installation costs can include site preparation, forming, pouring, finishing and other labor required to complete the driveway.

Site Preparation

Removing an existing driveway, excavation, grading, leveling and preparing the base can increase the total project cost.

Driveway Finish

A basic concrete finish generally costs less than decorative finishes. Stamping, coloring, textures and other design options can increase the project price.

Location

Concrete and labor prices can vary between states and local markets. Delivery distance and local contractor rates can also influence the final price.

Concrete Driveway Cost by Size

Single-Car Driveway

A smaller single-car driveway generally requires less concrete and labor than a larger two-car driveway.

Two-Car Driveway

A two-car driveway typically covers more square footage, which increases the amount of concrete and installation work required.

Large or Extended Driveway

Long or wide driveways can require significantly more concrete. Site access and preparation can also become more important as project size increases.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete driveway cost?

The cost depends on driveway size, thickness, material, labor, site preparation, location and finish. Use the calculator above to create a project-specific estimate.

How much does a concrete driveway cost per square foot?

The cost per square foot varies depending on the project and local market. Material, labor, preparation and finish can all affect the final price.

How thick should a concrete driveway be?

The appropriate thickness depends on the intended use, vehicle loads, soil conditions and local construction requirements. A qualified contractor can recommend the appropriate specification for your project.

Does replacing an existing driveway cost more?

It can. Removing an existing driveway, hauling away debris, excavating and preparing the base can add to the project cost.

Does location affect driveway prices?

Yes. Concrete material, delivery and labor costs can vary between states and local markets.

Are concrete driveway cost estimates exact?

No. Online calculators provide planning estimates rather than contractor quotes. Actual costs depend on local conditions, materials, labor, access and project specifications.

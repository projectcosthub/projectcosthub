How Our Roofing Cost Calculator Works

This calculator provides a general planning estimate based on roof area, roofing material, location and project complexity.

Roof Size

The total roof area is one of the most important factors when estimating roofing project costs. Larger roofs generally require more materials and installation work.

Roofing Material

Different roofing materials have different material and installation costs. Asphalt shingles are generally less expensive than metal or tile roofing.

Location

Material, labor and contractor costs can vary between states and local markets.

Project Complexity

Roof shape, pitch, accessibility, existing roofing and other project conditions can affect installation requirements.

Important:

This calculator provides a general estimate and is not a contractor quote. Actual costs can vary depending on roof condition, local labor rates, materials, accessibility, removal requirements, permits and project specifications.

How Much Does a New Roof Cost?

The cost of a new roof depends on the roof size, material, labor, location, roof complexity and condition of the existing roof.

Roof replacement projects may also include removal of the existing roofing, disposal, repairs, underlayment, flashing, ventilation and other work.

What Affects Roofing Cost?

Roof Area

A larger roof generally requires more roofing materials and more installation work.

Roofing Material

Asphalt shingles, metal roofing, tile roofing and flat roofing systems can have substantially different costs.

Roof Condition

Damage to decking, structural issues or other repairs can increase the final project cost.

Roof Removal

Removing existing roofing materials and disposing of them can add labor and disposal costs.

Roof Design

Complex roof shapes, multiple levels, steep slopes and difficult access can increase installation requirements.

Location

Local labor rates, material prices, permits and market conditions can affect roofing costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new roof cost?

The cost depends on roof size, material, labor, location, roof complexity and the condition of the existing roof. Use the calculator above for a general planning estimate.

What is the cheapest roofing material?

Asphalt shingles are commonly considered one of the more budget-friendly roofing options, although actual prices depend on product quality, installation and local market conditions.

Does roof size affect the cost?

Yes. Larger roofs generally require more materials and installation work.

Does location affect roofing prices?

Yes. Labor, materials, permits and other project costs can vary between states and local markets.

Are roofing calculator estimates exact?

No. The calculator provides a general planning estimate rather than a contractor quote. Actual costs should be confirmed with local roofing professionals.

Planning Tip:

Before starting a roofing project, compare several local contractor quotes and confirm the exact materials, removal work, repairs, warranties and other services included in each quote.

How Much Does a Concrete Patio Cost?

The cost of a concrete patio can vary significantly depending on the size of the patio, concrete prices, labor, site preparation, location and the type of finish selected.

A basic patio may cost less than a larger decorative patio with extensive preparation and finishing work. Because every property and project is different, a single price cannot accurately represent every concrete patio.

For this reason, it is useful to calculate the project based on square footage and then consider the local cost of materials and labor.

Concrete Patio Cost Per Square Foot

Contractors commonly consider concrete patio projects based on the cost per square foot. However, the actual price can vary depending on the project specifications.

The cost per square foot may be affected by concrete thickness, reinforcement, site preparation, accessibility, labor rates and the selected finish.

For example, a simple rectangular patio on an accessible, level site may be less expensive than a project that requires excavation, demolition or extensive grading.

Factors That Affect Concrete Patio Cost

Patio Size

The size of the patio is one of the most important factors affecting the amount of concrete required. A larger patio requires more material and generally requires more labor.

Concrete Thickness

A thicker patio slab requires more concrete than a thinner slab of the same size. The appropriate thickness depends on the intended use and project requirements.

Site Preparation

Preparing the area may include removing an existing surface, excavating soil, leveling the site, installing a suitable base and preparing the area for the concrete pour.

Projects requiring substantial preparation may have higher labor costs than a straightforward installation.

Concrete Finish

A basic concrete finish is generally simpler than decorative options. Stamped concrete, coloring, special textures and other decorative treatments can increase the cost.

Location

Local material and labor costs can vary significantly. Delivery distance, supplier availability and local contractor rates can all influence the final price.

Example: 200 Square Foot Concrete Patio

Consider a rectangular patio measuring 20 feet by 10 feet. The project would cover approximately 200 square feet.

If the slab is 4 inches thick, the project would require approximately 2.47 cubic yards of concrete before accounting for additional material or waste.

Concrete Patio vs. Other Patio Materials

Concrete is one of several materials that can be used for an outdoor patio. Other options include pavers, natural stone, brick and gravel.

The best choice depends on the desired appearance, budget, maintenance requirements, climate and intended use of the space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 200 square foot concrete patio cost?

The cost depends on material prices, labor, location, thickness, preparation and finish. Use the Project Cost Hub calculator to create a general estimate based on your specific project.

How thick should a concrete patio be?

The appropriate thickness depends on the intended use, ground conditions and project requirements. Homeowners should discuss the specifications with a qualified local contractor before construction.

Is a concrete patio expensive?

The total cost depends on the size and complexity of the project. A simple patio may be less expensive than a large project requiring demolition, excavation, grading or decorative finishing.

Can I install a concrete patio myself?

Small concrete projects may be possible for experienced DIY homeowners, but concrete installation can require specialized equipment, careful preparation and significant labor. Larger projects may be better suited to professionals.

Does location affect concrete patio prices?

Yes. Material prices, delivery costs and local labor rates can vary between states and local markets.

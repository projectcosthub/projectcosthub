How Much Concrete Do I Need?

Our concrete calculator estimates the volume of concrete needed based on the length, width and depth of your project.

1. Measure the project

Measure the length and width in feet. Measure the depth in inches.

2. Calculate cubic feet

The depth is converted from inches to feet. The length, width and depth are then multiplied to determine the volume in cubic feet.

3. Convert to cubic yards

Ready-mix concrete is commonly ordered by the cubic yard. One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet.

4. Add a waste allowance

A small additional amount can help account for measurement differences, spillage and uneven surfaces.

Example:

A 20 ft × 10 ft area with a depth of 4 inches requires approximately 2.47 cubic yards of concrete before adding a waste allowance.

Concrete Calculator FAQ

How much concrete do I need for a 10×10 slab?

The amount depends on the thickness of the slab. For example, a 10×10 foot slab that is 4 inches thick requires approximately 1.23 cubic yards before waste.

How much extra concrete should I order?

Many projects include an additional allowance for waste and measurement differences. The appropriate amount can depend on the shape and conditions of the project.

Should I order concrete by cubic yards?

Ready-mix concrete is commonly sold by the cubic yard. For larger projects, confirm the final quantity with your concrete supplier or contractor.

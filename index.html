How Does the Concrete Calculator Work?

Our concrete calculator estimates the amount of concrete needed based on the length, width and depth of your project.

Step 1: Measure your project

Measure the length and width in feet and the depth in inches.

Step 2: Calculate the volume

The calculator converts your measurements into cubic yards, which is the common unit used when ordering ready-mix concrete.

Step 3: Add a waste allowance

We add 10% to the calculated amount to provide a small allowance for waste and measurement differences.

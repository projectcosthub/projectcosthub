Roofing Cost Guide

Learn about the factors that can affect the cost of a roofing project, including materials, labor, roof size, location and replacement requirements.

How Much Does a New Roof Cost?

The cost of a new roof depends on several factors, including the size of the roof, roofing material, labor, roof design, location, accessibility and the condition of the existing roof.

A smaller roofing project may cost several thousand dollars, while a larger roof replacement can cost significantly more depending on the materials and project requirements.

What Affects Roofing Costs?

Roof Size

Larger roofs generally require more roofing materials and more installation labor. Roof size is therefore one of the most important factors when estimating a roofing project.

Roofing Material

Different roofing materials have different material and installation costs. Common options include asphalt shingles, metal roofing, tile and other roofing systems.

Roof Design and Complexity

A simple roof can be easier to install than a roof with multiple slopes, valleys, dormers, skylights or other architectural features.

Roof Pitch

Steeper roofs can require additional safety equipment, specialized installation methods and more labor.

Roof Removal

Removing an existing roof can add to the total project cost. The amount of old roofing material, number of existing layers and disposal requirements can affect the price.

Roof Deck Condition

If the underlying roof deck is damaged, repairs or replacement may be required before the new roofing system is installed.

Location

Roofing material prices, labor rates, disposal costs and local market conditions can vary between states and local areas.

Accessibility

Difficult access to a property can increase labor and equipment requirements. Multi-story homes and restricted work areas may require additional planning.

Roof Replacement vs. Roof Repair

A roof repair may be appropriate when the damage is limited to a small area or a specific roofing component. A full replacement may be necessary when the roof has widespread damage, is near the end of its expected service life or has significant underlying problems.

The appropriate solution depends on the condition of the roof and should be evaluated by a qualified roofing professional.

Common Roofing Materials

Asphalt Shingles

Asphalt shingles are a common residential roofing material and are available in different quality levels and styles.

Metal Roofing

Metal roofing can offer durability and a different appearance from traditional asphalt shingles. Material and installation costs can vary depending on the type of metal roofing selected.

Tile Roofing

Tile roofing can provide a distinctive appearance and long service life, but the material and installation requirements can be different from asphalt roofing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a roof replacement cost?

Roof replacement costs vary based on roof size, material, labor, roof complexity, location and removal requirements. Use the roofing calculator above for a general planning estimate.

What is the cheapest roofing material?

Material prices vary by market and project. Asphalt shingles are commonly used for residential roofing and can be available at different price levels.

Does roof size affect the cost?

Yes. Larger roofs generally require more materials and labor, which can increase the total project cost.

Does roof pitch affect roofing costs?

Yes. Steeper roofs can require additional safety measures, equipment and labor.

Does removing an old roof increase the cost?

Yes. Removing and disposing of existing roofing materials can add labor and disposal costs to a replacement project.

Can damaged roof decking increase the cost?

Yes. If the underlying roof deck requires repairs or replacement, those additional materials and labor can increase the total project cost.

Does location affect roofing prices?

Yes. Roofing labor, materials, delivery, disposal and local market conditions can vary significantly by location.

Are roofing calculator estimates exact?

No. Online calculators provide general planning estimates rather than contractor quotes. Actual project costs depend on the specific property, materials, labor and site conditions.

Planning Tip:

Before starting a roofing project, compare several local contractor quotes and confirm the roofing materials, removal work, repairs, installation details and warranties included in each quote.
